🌸 A Birthday Letter 🌸

My dearest Arebah,

Happiest birthday to you! Today is not just another day on the calendar. It is a day to celebrate the existence of someone genuinely special. Someone whose presence makes ordinary days a little brighter and whose friendship is a blessing in its own beautiful way.

😂 To My Asthmatic Partner

Dear Arebah,

First of all, happy birthday to the one person whose asthma deserves its own attendance record! 😂

I genuinely hope your birthday comes with unlimited laughter, cake and absolutely no unnecessary wheezing. May your lungs cooperate with you for once instead of behaving like they have their own plans. Please remember that even your inhaler deserves a birthday break!

Jokes aside, I really want you to know how wonderful you are. You are genuinely one of those people who deserve to be appreciated more often. There is something special about the way you carry yourself, the way you interact with people and the person you are.

You are truly a Child of God. I mean that sincerely. You have so much potential, goodness and beauty within you. I hope you never underestimate the person you are becoming. You deserve kindness, opportunities, blessings and every beautiful thing life has to offer.

I also want to thank you for being my partner during our O&G posting. Those days would have been different without you. Thank you for sharing that experience with me, for being there through the posting, and for making the journey more memorable.

Clinical postings can be tiring, stressful and unpredictable. There are days when everything feels overwhelming, but having someone to share the experience with makes such a difference. I genuinely appreciate the memories we created together.

And before I finish, I want to apologise if I have ever knowingly or unknowingly hurt you, upset you, made you uncomfortable or said something that did not come across the right way. Sometimes we may not realise how our words or actions affect someone else. If I have done anything like that, I am genuinely sorry.

I never want a careless word or an unintended action to overshadow the good memories and friendship we have shared. Thank you for being part of my journey.

Stay the wonderful person you are. Keep smiling, keep growing, keep being kind and, most importantly, take care of those lungs! 😂

Your O&G partner,
Always wishing you the best 🌿

🩺 A Letter From Your Future

Dear Dr Arebah,

I want you to imagine yourself a few years from now. Walking into a hospital with confidence, wearing your professional attire, knowing that you have worked incredibly hard to reach the position you are standing in.

Because I genuinely believe that you are going to become an amazing doctor. Not just someone who holds a medical degree, but someone who makes a real difference in the lives of patients.

And especially, I can already imagine you becoming one of the best anaesthesiologists around! The one who knows exactly what to do, remains calm during challenging situations, understands every detail and earns the trust of colleagues and patients alike.

One day, I hope people will proudly recommend you because of your knowledge, your skills, your dedication and the quality of care you provide. I hope you become the kind of doctor whom juniors look up to and patients feel safe around.

And of course, we cannot forget the financial side of things! 😂 I wish you become the richest anaesthesiologist in the room. May your bank account be as impressive as your medical skills. May you earn enough to travel, experience the world, spoil yourself, support your family and enjoy all the things you once dreamed about.

There will undoubtedly be difficult days. There may be exhausting calls, long hours, challenging patients and moments when you question yourself. But please remember that every great doctor was once a student who was still learning.

Do not allow temporary setbacks to make you doubt your permanent potential. Be patient with yourself. Keep learning, keep improving and never lose the compassion that makes medicine meaningful.

I hope you look back one day and realise that all those little efforts, sacrifices and difficult moments were building the person you always wanted to become.

So, future Dr Arebah, go out there and make it happen. Become excellent, become successful, become wealthy and, most importantly, remain a good human being.

But today? Forget about all the responsibilities! It is your birthday. Eat cake, laugh loudly, enjoy yourself and have the best time possible.

The hospital can wait. The future anaesthesiologist deserves a proper birthday celebration! 😂

To your incredibly bright future,
Your friend 🌿
